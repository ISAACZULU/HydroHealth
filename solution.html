Our Solution

A dual-purpose approach that addresses both flooding and water contamination simultaneously

The Dual-Purpose Approach

Our innovative system tackles two critical problems with one integrated solution.

💧

Engineered Drainage

Our channels improve surface water flow, preventing the pooling that breeds disease and damages homes. By directing stormwater away from living areas, we reduce flood risk and protect infrastructure.

🌿

Multi-Layer Filtration

Using gravel, sand, charcoal, and mesh, we naturally filter contaminants from stormwater before it reaches the community. This reduces waterborne pathogens and improves overall water quality.

How It Works

Our four-step process transforms flood-prone areas into safer, healthier communities.

1

Identify

We work with community members to map flood-prone zones and identify priority areas through surveys and peer-led interviews. This ensures our interventions target the most critical needs.

2

Construct

Local teams build filtration drainage channels using affordable, locally-sourced materials like gravel, sand, charcoal, and perforated pipes. This creates jobs and ensures community ownership.

3

Filter

As stormwater flows through the channels, each layer traps solid waste, silt, and harmful pathogens, cleaning the water naturally before it reaches community areas.

4

Sustain

Trained youth teams maintain the systems, conduct awareness campaigns, and monitor health outcomes with local clinics. This ensures long-term sustainability and continuous improvement.

Technical Design

The science behind our filtration drainage system.

Layer 1: Coarse Gravel

Large stones capture debris and solid waste, preventing blockages and protecting subsequent filtration layers.

Layer 2: Fine Sand

Medium-grade sand filters smaller particles and sediments, removing suspended solids from the water flow.

Layer 3: Activated Charcoal

Charcoal absorbs organic contaminants, odors, and some chemicals, providing additional water purification.

Layer 4: Fine Mesh

Wire mesh captures remaining fine particles while allowing water to flow through freely.

Perforated Pipes

Underground pipes direct filtered water away from living areas, preventing pooling and facilitating proper drainage.

Vegetative Cover

Plants help stabilize the soil, absorb excess water, and provide natural beauty to the installation sites.

Community-Led Implementation

Our approach ensures sustainability by putting the community at the center of every project.

👷

Local Employment

We train and employ local youth to construct and maintain the systems, creating jobs and building valuable skills.

🎓

Skills Training

🤝

Community Ownership

By involving residents in planning and implementation, we ensure the systems are valued and maintained by the community.
