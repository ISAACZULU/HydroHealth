Current Impact

The measurable change we're creating in Kabanana and beyond.

Environmental Impact

  • ✓ Reduced stagnant water and flooding in target areas
  • ✓ Cleaner runoff entering local ecosystems
  • ✓ Prevention of soil erosion
  • ✓ Reduced greenhouse gas emissions from debris burning

Health Impact

  • ✓ Fewer cases of cholera, typhoid, and malaria
  • ✓ Safer walking paths for families
  • ✓ Protection of water sources from contamination

Social Impact

  • ✓ Youth employment and skills training
  • ✓ Women's burden reduced
  • ✓ Community ownership and pride

Our Roadmap for Expansion

Our vision for scaling impact across Zambia and beyond.

Phase 1: Kabanana (Current)

Establish proof of concept in Kabanana, documenting impact and refining the model for replication.

Phase 2: Lusaka Expansion

Replicate the solution in other informal settlements across Lusaka, adapting designs to local conditions.

Phase 3: National Scale

Partner with government agencies and NGOs to implement the solution in flood-prone communities across Zambia.

Phase 4: Regional & Global

Share the model with organizations working in similar contexts across Sub-Saharan Africa and the Global South.
